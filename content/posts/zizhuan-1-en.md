<p>Scatter notes after NHSML '26</p>
<p>(This is topic 2)</p>
<p>Zhao Zihan, C8G2</p>
<p>On September 14, 2026, the classroom of C3G2 was empty again, and we felt empty too. We didn't know whether we had to leave.
I probably know why those people leave, it's a feeling called hopelessness (except those big shots like Miao, who can "get provincial first prize at dawn, then quit it at dusk"). They've prepared it so long, walking into the exam room confidentially yet coming out lost in the end. They thought it was funny, so they left without looking back.</p>
<p>In my life, I always see geniuses, who are glamorous and extraordinary, while I only have rust. I always want to prove that I can, just like chickens and ducks yearn for the sky. So I took part in many competitions, trying to prove myself with awards. But the more you learn, the more lost; the more you learn, the more irritable — Wu AC-ed the problem in 10 minutes while I had to think for hours; Miao said when I had only done less than half of it: "I've AK-ed (got a full score)" — I learn from them every day and make them my goals, but I am also aware that I am not the same as them.</p>
<p>Recently, I read Zaoxue. I have got a general understanding of the ideas of the two Confucian and Taoistic families. I've understand the benevolence and righteousness of Confucianism for a while, but I can't do anything about governing the country and bringing peace to the world for a while. So I like the idea of Taoism better. I feel that their thoughts are similar to those of Mr. Zheng Bochen. — "Is the math competition just for scoring? Just for the 15 points in the college entrance examination, the 30 points in the high school league. Or for the so-called provincial team? — Don't be too narrow. If you don't want to lay the foundation for college mathematics, it is based on your love for this, and you shouldn't give up just because of one exam." The corresponding Taoist literature is the story of Huizi getting a gourd in "The Journey of the Wandering" (Xiaoyaoyou). I think: learning mathematics and carrying this gourd are essentially the same. We don't need to ask ourselves why we should continue learning, we love it, so we learn it.</p>
<p>In fact, the Confucian literature has similar ideas. I was very puzzled when I learned "Sitting with Confucius" in my first year of high school. Why did the words of Zeng Dian point be misinterpreted as a concrete manifestation of a harmonious society? In my opinion, "feel the wind on the stage and go back home singing" is also a manifestation of personal carefreeness. He doesn't care about what achievements he has made like Zhong You or Ran You. That calmness of his character. This is what we modern people should pursue.</p>
<p>Someone asked, do I want to be a skilled person like Miao? This question is very complicated. Not to mention whether I can reach such great heights, for Miao himself, he does not have a 100% chance of getting AK in every exam, he will also fail. And after we have acquired the character of composure, whether he has the superpower, it is not so important.</p>
<p>The league ended, some people failed due to the change of the order, and some lost to the proudest geometry. And I hope that I will no longer care about what awards I win, but take a deep breath after the test bell rings. Someone patted me on the shoulder — the world is brilliant and grand, welcome home.</p>
